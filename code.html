Online Dice Roller

DiceRoll is a fast, realistic way to roll one to four six-sided dice directly in your browser. Choose how many dice you need, press Roll Dice, and watch them tumble, collide and settle naturally inside the virtual dice tray. Just like real dice, the number facing upward after each die stops determines the result. DiceRoll automatically reads those values and calculates the total for you.

How a Six-Sided Die Works

A standard six-sided die (often abbreviated as D6) has six numbered faces marked from 1 to 6. A classic rule of authentic cubical dice geometry is:

"Opposite faces on a standard six-sided die add up to seven."

1 ↔ 6
1 + 6 = 7
2 ↔ 5
2 + 5 = 7
3 ↔ 4
3 + 4 = 7

Dice Probability

For a fair D6, each face has exactly a 1 in 6 (~16.67%) chance. When rolling two dice together, totals range from 2 to 12. Because multiple combinations can produce middle numbers, 7 is the single most common total with 6 out of 36 combinations (16.67%).

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the dice results random? expand_more
Yes. Each roll produces a new result and the physical 3D dice determine the final visible values.
How is the dice result determined? expand_more
After the dice finish moving, DiceRoll reads the face pointing upward on each die. Those values become the result and are added together.
Can I roll more than one die? expand_more
Yes. DiceRoll supports between one and four six-sided dice.
Can I use DiceRoll on my phone? expand_more
Yes. DiceRoll is designed for desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
What does D6 mean? expand_more
D6 means a six-sided die with values from 1 to 6.
Do opposite sides of a die add up to seven? expand_more
On a standard D6, yes: 1 opposite 6, 2 opposite 5, 3 opposite 4.
