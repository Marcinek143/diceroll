The six `dice-impact-*.wav` files are short impact excerpts from Kenney's
**Casino Audio (1.1)** pack, available at
https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips.

They were trimmed, mixed to mono, normalized, and faded at the edges for use
with individual physics contacts. The source recordings are released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0). See `LICENSE-KENNEY.txt` for the pack's license.
